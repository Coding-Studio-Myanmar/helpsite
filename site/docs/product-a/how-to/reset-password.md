## Before you begin

- You need access to the email address on your account.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and select the link.
4. Enter and confirm your new password.

:::tip
Use a passphrase of 12 or more characters.
:::

## Troubleshooting

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder, then see [FAQ & Troubleshooting](../faq/index.md).
