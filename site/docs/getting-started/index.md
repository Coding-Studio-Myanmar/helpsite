Welcome to the **Coding Studio Knowledge Base**. Everything here is public: no sign-in required.

## Where to find things

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Do a specific task step by step | **How-To Guides** inside your product |
| Understand how something works or integrate with it | **Technical Documentation** inside your product |
| Learn a product from scratch | **Training Guides** |
| Fix a problem | **FAQ & Troubleshooting** inside your product |

Use the **search box** at the top of every page to find an article by keyword.

## Getting help

Can't find what you need? Contact the Coding Studio support team through your usual support channel.
